Our approach

Upright uses your device's camera and on-device pose detection (MediaPipe) to measure how your head, neck, and shoulders are positioned in real time — no data leaves your device. It scores posture out of 10 and matches exercises to the specific pattern it detects, instead of giving one-size-fits-all advice.
